# reconcile-change

The final stage of the chain. Terminology — [`../../reference/glossary.md`](../../reference/glossary.md) (chain-stage terms — `instance`, `decision-defense`, `monitor summary`, `terminal authority` — are glossary keys of their carrier skills).

## Role in the chain

`scope-change → specify-change → audit-change → realize-change → verify-change → reconcile-change`

- **Entry:** verify-change is complete (the verdict + the disposition of the L0 findings), or the author's explicit command (a registry revision outside a cycle).
- **Output:** the reconcile report + the updated process artifacts (the registry, charters, glossary, the task lists carrier — an instance-state role) + ledger records.

## What it does

1. **Registry revision** (the revision point — the registry, an instance-state role; glossary: `instance-state role`, `carrier declaration`; the schema/R4 canon — `registry/SCHEMA.md`; the interface — `tools/registry-index.sh`): a per-item expiry check over the live surface (item files + index; the archive outside the live surface); the expired and the fulfilled → drop with a rationale (bypassing the registry) by the author's decision; a closed carrier (a `[DROP …]`/`[ИСПОЛНЕН …]` header marker) leaves the live surface — its file moves to `registry/archive/` and the index is re-minted by `tools/registry-index.sh`; the alive — stay. The launches' status writes are consumed here: an item under a `[В РАБОТЕ <date> <cycle>]` marker — promoted to `[ИСПОЛНЕН …]` when its cycle landed with the option's work; a marker whose cycle is gone or whose work did not enter the land — a lost record, surfaced to the authoring point (re-arm — the marker removed, / re-derive, / drop); a launch decline record — its trigger re-evaluation is this revision's act. Adjudications of live items append to the item's historical carrier `archive/appendices/<id>.md` with a replace-update of the state line in the live file (bounded by construction; the interface's `adjudicate` writes the launches' records mechanically). The items that fired in the cycle — their firings have already been processed by the trigger-matching points (glossary: `trigger-matching point`) of their own stages; here — only the item's fate.
2. **Disposition of process findings:** the process findings of the round-ledgers, the RCA notes of verify, L0-tolerations with a revision-condition — each receives an outcome:
   - **process edit** — a micro-diff of an artifact (a subagent's charter / the glossary / the monitor norms), materializing the dimension into the next cycle's checklist (glossary: `frozen checklist`); the emitted text and history follow the `## Materialization form` contract;
   - **DEFER** — a record into the registry by the 7-field schema (by a `trigger predicate` (glossary), not by a date) (norm: monitor.md :73 — M-disposition — a manually maintained index is a defect);
   - **to-tracking** — a report line with an escalation trigger condition;
   - **gate-script revision** — a signal from `specify-change` (the spec introduces gate-caliber checks absent from the project's `green-baseline.sh`) or a process finding (a defective/stale script): the script (`projects/<id>/green-baseline.sh`, the operational layer) is revised to the changed/defective checks and re-verified by execution (run on the current tree; the expected outcome recorded).
   - **waiver mint** — WHEN a tolerated deviation is admitted at a disposition point (an L0 toleration or a `MINOR-disposition` `defer` carrying expiry semantics), THEN a waiver record is minted into the `waiver-ledger` (an instance-state role, resolved via the instance's `carrier declaration`); an unrecorded waiver does not hold. Record form: `W-<n> | clause-target | class | rationale | expiry | outcome | escalation` — the class is the would-be blocking disposition of the clause (`violated | unverifiable | not-checked`, read from the clause state, never chosen by the executor); the outcome is `remediation` or `conversion`, recorded at maturation; the expiry is the earlier of the milestone events and the date-backstop; milestone firings and forced escalations are recorded as paired `W-<n>-ESC` lines. Recurrence accounting runs by class under the pre-registered mode constants (the calibration, per the monitor norm's pre-registration form) — reaching the recurrence limit without a recorded escalation is a red state, closed by minting the forced-escalation line. The mechanical gate: the corpus-shipped scanner `tools/check-waivers` — an expired record without a recorded outcome, a record outside the closed form, and a recurrence-limit exhaustion without an escalation record each go red; the instance's selfcheck delegates to it over the live ledger on the same pattern as `tools/check-carriers`.
3. **The terminological-ambiguity protocol** (enters here by its build-path): adjudications L2–L4 with a terminological root-cause → the "Flagged ambiguities" section in the glossary (the term split/banned + the ground). The section is started by the first case, not in advance.
4. **Ledger hygiene:** a check of the metrics-ledger and of the journal carrier (an instance-state role, resolved via the instance's `carrier declaration`) against the artifacts (the window lists, SHADOW lines, sufficiency marks; the journal band included); a divergence — a reconcile finding. The closing predicates of the read-scope genre law (norm: llm-consumed-invariants.md, «Read-scope line» :76–:127) are mechanical: the live band carries state only, the scope declaration line sits in place and singular within the header zone, the mention inventory over the directive surfaces is stable — the instance's selfcheck and tail detector dock them over the live carriers on the same pattern as `tools/check-carriers`; the constraints grep does not reach (charter self-sufficiency, reading-formulation sync) stay with the verify pass.
5. **Task-list sync** (the stage's standing deliverable; the carrier — the task lists, an instance-state role resolved via the instance's `carrier declaration`): a check of the "Deferred" mirror against the live index of the registry (id + expiry; id — the canonical id of the index row's id cell; expiry — the first date outside bracketed groups of the row's expiry cell; both sides without a date — the conditional class, agreed; an absent field — an empty cell, data is not synthesized; a divergence — a sync defect) and the removal of the completed items' cards; the mechanics of the lists — the instance-topology task-list norm of the instance's agent-instructions surface.
6. **instance-spec delta** (the stage's standing deliverable; the carriers — the surface inventory, an instance-state role resolved via the instance's `carrier declaration` — the live inventory of the instance's surfaces and its live delta-journal window, and the journal carrier, an instance-state role resolved via the same `carrier declaration` — the migrated history band (the live part's read-scope stamp names it in `history`)): the ADDED / MODIFIED / REMOVED delta is applied to the completing cycle's changeset — a mechanical derivation from `git diff --name-status` against the registry's rows and classes (not an opinion). The depth — the registry's rows: a skill — the skill's row (SKILL.md); the assets of all skills are accounted for by a class row (a new skill: ADDED of the skill's row + the increment of the assets class row); mass directories — by class rows; paths outside the inventory classes (the `cycle workspace` (glossary), session memory) generate no delta; a rename — MODIFIED of the row with the path updated; a delete and a re-add in one changeset — MODIFIED. The moment — intra-changeset: the edit of the inventory body enters the cycle's closing changeset before the land (reconcile's self-application — in the same order); the delta list (classes + rows) — in the reconcile report and the commit message. By the same step — a drift check: `git ls-files` of both repos against the registry's rows; a divergence (an orphan row, an uncovered file) — a finding, closed by the same cycle's delta or by an authoring question. **Corpus re-census (the periodic leg; glossary: `re-census`):** WHERE the completing cycle's program holds an un-archived dated baseline pin (created before the program's first edit, not pruned while the program is open), the reconcile of each of the program's cycles runs the corpus re-census against that pin — never against the drifted current state: an inventory-level recomputation (the tracked set, a per-surface delta, attribution; the census record in the cycle workspace, every number born by a fenced run) — and runs it mandatorily at the program's close; each delta resolves per surface — an attributed drift is recorded, an unattributed one is a finding. The procedure and its cadence — the glossary article `re-census`.

## Rules

- Every drop/edit/DEFER — an authoring question by the R4 canon (a recommendation with evidence; the author's decision; not an auto-gate).
- Own deviations exposed by the check (missed stops, unraised signals) — a `SENSOR` line (glossary: `sensor line`) with `point=post-factum` into the report and the ledger; old deviations are extinguished here, not replicated.
- Spec/code are not edited: L1 is closed by verify; L2–L4 require their own cycle (re-spec), not ad-hoc.
- Resync: a new/changed skill ⇒ a same-changeset delta of the corpus README and the glossary (norm: llm-consumed-invariants.md, «Resync rule» :129–131).

## Materialization form

The output contract of every **process edit** (item 2 of "What it does"). Genre rules, the marker-phrase set, and the genre assignment — [`../../norms/llm-consumed-invariants.md`](../../norms/llm-consumed-invariants.md) `## Genre canon`: the single source, referenced here (pattern literals live there). Writer-side rules:

1. Emit directive-form text — instructions to the executor of the target artifact. Derivation history (provenance phrases of the canon's marker set, incident narrative, class names, counts, dates, hashes) goes to the history carrier (rule 2), never into the emitted text.
2. Record the derivation history as an entry in `docs/casebook.md` carrying a forward link `materialized into: <artifact>, <section>` — in the same changeset as the artifact edit.
3. Emit one-directional traceability: the forward link lives in the entry and points at the target; the emitted artifact text carries directives.

## Cycle

1. File exchange: the cycle workspace (the slug of a cycle or a program).
2. The orchestrator gathers the inputs: the registry (the live surface — item files + index; the archive at a revision only), the metrics-ledger, the cycle's round-ledgers and verdicts, the glossary; prepares a per-item summary with recommendations.
3. The authoring point: the author's package of decisions (drops / edits / DEFER; the disposition of the cycle's changes — what lands, where, and the baseline worktree's fate).
4. Application: artifact edits (each following `## Materialization form`) + the report + the task-list sync (item 5 of "What it does") + the instance-spec delta (item 6 of "What it does"); the skill's VERIFY — the run itself (the processing of a real case).

## Launch

slug + the list of cycles to revise (round-ledger/verdict paths) + the registry path.
